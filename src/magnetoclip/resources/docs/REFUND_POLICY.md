# MagnetoClip Refund Policy

**Effective date:** September 5, 2026

This Refund Policy ("**Policy**") describes the circumstances under which
Devallix ("**Devallix**", "**we**", "**us**", or "**our**") will issue a refund
for a paid license to the MagnetoClip software ("**MagnetoClip**" or the
"**Software**"). This Policy is part of, and should be read together with, the
[MagnetoClip Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md) and the
[MagnetoClip End User License Agreement](EULA.md).

---

## 1. The 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

**1.1.** We want you to be completely satisfied with MagnetoClip. If you
purchase a MagnetoClip license and are not satisfied for any reason, you may
request a full refund of the purchase price within 30 days of the date you
purchased the license (the "**Refund Period**").

**1.2.** The guarantee applies to licenses purchased directly from Devallix,
including through the MagnetoClip website or any official store we operate. For
a license purchased from an authorized reseller or third-party store, please
request the refund from that reseller under its own policy; we may not be able
to process it directly.

## 2. Eligibility

**2.1.** To be eligible for a refund under this Policy, you must:

**(a)** have purchased the license directly from Devallix or the MagnetoClip
website;

**(b)** submit your request before the Refund Period ends; and

**(c)** not be associated with fraudulent, abusive, or repeated refund activity.

**2.2.** Refunds are issued to the original payment method whenever possible, or
to a method we choose consistent with the applicable payment provider's rules.
Taxes and fees collected with the purchase are refunded to the extent permitted
by your payment provider and applicable law.

## 3. What Is Not Covered

**3.1.** Licenses purchased more than 30 days before the date of your request.

**3.2.** Gifted, promotional, or otherwise unpaid licenses and activations.

**3.3.** License renewals where the Refund Period has already ended.

**3.4.** Licenses purchased from a third-party reseller or store (see
Section 1.2).

**3.5.** Nothing in this Policy limits any statutory consumer rights you may
have under the laws of your country; where such rights apply, they prevail.

## 4. How to Request a Refund

**4.1.** Contact Devallix through the support channels provided with the Software
or on the MagnetoClip website. To speed up processing, include:

**(a)** the email address or account used to purchase the license;

**(b)** your license or order reference, if available;

**(c)** the date of purchase; and

**(d)** a brief reason for the request.

**4.2.** Requests submitted within the Refund Period are reviewed and confirmed.
You will receive a response confirming whether the refund is approved.

## 5. After Approval

**5.1.** We aim to process approved refunds within 5–10 business days of
confirmation. The refund will appear in your account according to your payment
provider's processing times.

**5.2.** On approval, the license is revoked and deactivated. You must stop using
and uninstall MagnetoClip. Your local data — including download history and the
files you have already saved to your computer — remains yours and is not
affected by the refund.

**5.3.** After a refund you are free to purchase a new license in the future,
subject to this Policy.

## 6. Contact

**6.1.** If you have any questions about this Policy, or to submit a refund
request, contact Devallix through the support channels provided with the
Software or on the MagnetoClip website.
